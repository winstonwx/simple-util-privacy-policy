Privacy Policy for Buy Check

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Buy Check ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Buy Check is designed to help users evaluate purchases and build better spending habits. We do not directly collect, store, or sell personal information.

Information you enter into the app—such as purchase prices, income values, savings goals, spending notes, and skipped purchase history—is stored locally on your device.

2. Local Data Storage

Your analysis history, savings progress, dashboard statistics, and app preferences are stored on your device only.

We do not upload your personal financial inputs or purchase records to external servers.

3. Advertising (Free Version)

If ads are enabled in Buy Check, we may use Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address (for general location)
  • App usage information related to ad performance

This information is collected by Google for ad delivery and analytics.

Google Privacy Policy

4. In-App Purchases

Buy Check may offer optional purchases such as ad removal. Payments are securely processed by the platform provider (such as Google Play or Apple App Store).

We do not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Third-Party Services

Buy Check may use:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising, if enabled)
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase systems

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage ad personalization and permissions through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

7. Children's Privacy

Buy Check does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Third-party services may collect limited technical information as described in their own policies.

8. Data Security

Because Buy Check stores personal usage data locally on your device, you maintain control over your information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
