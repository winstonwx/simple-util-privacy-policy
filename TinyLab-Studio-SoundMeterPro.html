Privacy Policy for Sound Meter Pro

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Sound Meter Pro ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Sound Meter Pro is designed to measure and analyze environmental sound levels using your device microphone.

We do not directly collect, store, or sell personal information.

2. Microphone Permission

Sound Meter Pro requires microphone access to detect surrounding sound and calculate decibel (dB) levels.

Audio captured by the microphone is processed locally on your device in real time for measurement purposes.

We do not record, store, upload, or transmit audio to our servers.

3. Local Data Storage

App settings, calibration preferences, measurement history, and customization options may be stored locally on your device.

This information remains under your control and is not uploaded to external servers.

4. Advertising (Free Version)

If ads are enabled, Sound Meter Pro may use Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

AdMob may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address (for general location)
  • Ad interaction and performance data

This information is handled by Google according to Google's privacy policy.

Google Privacy Policy

5. In-App Purchases

Sound Meter Pro may offer optional purchases such as Remove Ads Forever.

Payments are securely processed by platform providers such as Google Play or Apple App Store.

We do not receive or store your payment card details.

6. Third-Party Services

Sound Meter Pro may use:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising, if enabled)
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase systems

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage ad personalization and app permissions through your device settings.

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Sound Meter Pro → Permissions
  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

8. Children's Privacy

Sound Meter Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Third-party services may collect limited technical information as described in their own policies.

9. Data Security

Since measurement data and app settings are stored locally on your device, you maintain control over your information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
