Privacy Policy for QR and Barcode Scanner

Last updated: May 23, 2026

QR and Barcode Scanner ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

QR and Barcode Scanner is designed to scan and process QR codes and barcodes directly on your device. We do not directly collect, store, or sell personal information.

Scanned QR codes, barcode history, favorites, and app settings are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

2. Camera Permission

The app requires camera access to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera data is processed in real time on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

3. Local Data Storage

Scan history, generated QR codes, favorites, and app preferences are stored locally on your device only.

We do not upload your scan history or personal data to external servers.

4. Internet Access

Some scanned QR codes or barcodes may contain website links or online content. The app may open external websites or perform online product lookups when requested by the user.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites and services.

5. Advertising (Free Version)

If ads are enabled in QR and Barcode Scanner, we may use Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address (for general location)
  • App usage information related to ad performance

This information is collected by Google for ad delivery and analytics.

Google Privacy Policy

6. In-App Purchases

QR and Barcode Scanner may offer optional purchases such as ad removal or premium features. Payments are securely processed by the platform provider (such as Google Play or Apple App Store).

We do not receive or store your payment card details.

7. Third-Party Services

QR and Barcode Scanner may use:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising, if enabled)
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase systems

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage app permissions and ad personalization through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Permission Manager / Ads
  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera / Tracking

9. Children's Privacy

QR and Barcode Scanner does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Third-party services may collect limited technical information as described in their own policies.

10. Data Security

Because QR and Barcode Scanner stores user data locally on your device, you maintain control over your information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
